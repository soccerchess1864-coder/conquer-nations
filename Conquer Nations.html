Conquer Nations

Pick your nation, the enemy bots' nation and a map, then press Play. Single player against bots, nothing to paste.

New: on Wake Island and Iwo Jima press E next to your ship to sail it (click = large cannon, hold right-click = machine gun). On any map with trenches press E at a mounted machine gun to man it. Bots use the guns too. Pick a gadget (bazooka, flamethrower or medkit) in the Loadout screen and use it on key 3 (knife is now V). On Wake Island bots stay out of the water and you swim slowly.
